Transportation & Logistics

Washing Machine Distribution Network Analysis

Role: Logistics Analyst

The Challenge: Analyzed the distribution network for washing machines from the Kudus DC. The goal was to transition from a high-cost, multi-modal convoy model to an optimized cluster-based system to reduce variable logistics costs.

  • Cost Modeling & Savings: Developed a dynamic cost calculator (Fuel, Labor, Tolls). The simulation projected a 26.93% potential cost saving by optimizing vehicle space utilization.
  • Algorithmic Planning: Applied Saving Matrix to group delivery points into efficient clusters (Cluster 1, 2, 3) and used Nearest Insert algorithms to design the final dispatch sequences.
  • Operational Impact: The final route output serves as the Dispatch Baseline for daily shipment tracking, ensuring On-Time Delivery (OTD) performance.
